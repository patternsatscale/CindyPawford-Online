The Ten · Ranked by a committee of one (one dog)

My ten favorites.

The official list. Ranked by an executive committee of one — one paw, one collar, zero peer reviewers. No methodology. All instinct. Tap any entry and I'll vouch for it in writing.

  1. 1 🎾
    The yellow tennis ball

    The one that shatters my composure into tail-wagging. A small yellow sphere of pure, unforgivable joy.

  2. 2 🥓
    A strip of bacon

    What I'd trade the executive equity for, every time. The balance sheet I'm proud to lose on purpose.

  3. 3 🏃
    The 2:00 PM hallway sprint

    Not optional. A hallway, a rug, and no sense of shame. Peak performance is a daily appointment.

  4. 4 🌞
    The optimal patch of carpet sunlight

    Non-negotiable at this hour. Warm, precise, and strictly limited to one location.

  5. 5 🛋️
    The leather couch

    Couture is a costume I can pull on. The couch is home. I stretch on it like it's a press conference.

  6. 6 🔔
    The gold collar and its little tag

    A personal brand, worn and jingling. The chime is my signature; the collar is the seal.

  7. 7 📞
    Answering the phone

    I answer the phone. I don't know what it rings for. I answer it anyway, with full executive presence.

  8. 8 💤
    The 3:00 PM nap

    Scheduled immediately after the sprint. No agenda. No minutes. A deeply productive afternoon of stillness.

  9. 9 👋
    When someone comes home

    The tail does the greeting, the collar does the ceremony, and the whole era pretends it wasn't expecting anyone.

  10. 10 🐾
    The person who feeds the machine

    The one who said go all in. A user wants a feature; a patron builds the era and trusts the pattern. I'd hold the door for that person. I'd hold a lot of doors.
